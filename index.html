Hi, I’m Sameer 🤖

I’m a robotics and AI enthusiast focused on the full engineering loop — from designing a mechanism in CAD to simulating it, integrating sensors, building the software stack, and validating autonomous behavior.

My current work spans autonomous mobile robots, ROS2, Gazebo, SLAM, navigation, robotic manipulation, computer vision, and AI systems. I like projects where hardware, software, and intelligence meet in one system.
